A contemporary sloping-roof villa at Chhattarpur. The 3D views show a corner house built up from several gabled volumes in charcoal and warm grey, their roofs tilted at different angles and lined with white soffits. Balconies are cut into the gables, a row of small gabled garage doors repeats the roof form at street level, and a low wall with planters and a line of cypress trees edges the plot. A line sketch studies the same corner, with its stepped roofs, by hand.
