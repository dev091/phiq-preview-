Jack n Jill CBSE School

Shivpuri, Madhya Pradesh

A school designed for the hot climate of Shivpuri, using multiple colours.

The school building has been designed keeping in consideration the majorly hot climatic conditions of Shivpuri, and it uses multiple colours. In the 3D views, the classroom blocks present facades of tall coloured panels in yellow, green, blue, violet and orange between grey and white walls, with framed windows. A central entrance under a steep A-frame canopy marks the arrival, reached by a drop-off loop around a sculptural centrepiece and an avenue lined with planting. A line sketch of the front elevation shows the same composition of blocks and canopy drawn by hand.
