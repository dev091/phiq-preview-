Interior design

Interiors for a 3 BHK apartment in Indore, designed in a contemporary style. The living room reads as one long view: a television wall of diagonal timber slats, a pale grey wall raised in a diamond pattern, brown sofas and a navy chaise around round glass and brass tables, and a lit tray ceiling hung with clusters of glass pendants. Screens with a gold leaf motif frame the passage to the dining area, where deep green walls and green quilted chairs face glass doors and a view over the city. The bedrooms keep the warmth: a tan leather headboard and a bed base edged in light in the boy's bedroom, and an upholstered chevron headboard with pink pendant lamps in the guest bedroom.
