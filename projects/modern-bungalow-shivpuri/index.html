A modern bungalow at Shivpuri. The 3D views show it at dusk: dark stone-clad volumes cantilevered over the entrance, a lit frame around the upper terrace, and a row of cypresses against the evening sky. The street elevation sets broad horizontal frames and glazing against a tall glazed stair core, behind a low wall planted with palms. Warm uplighting along the boundary wall and the garage gate completes the evening view, and the upper rooms open onto the terrace behind full-height glazing.
