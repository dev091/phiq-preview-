Modern House at Ujjain

Ujjain, Madhya Pradesh

A minimalist modern house in Ujjain.

A minimalist house in Ujjain, composed as a stack of clean volumes. In the photographs, grey and white plastered planes step back floor by floor: a deep first-floor terrace with a glass balustrade and planters, a roof terrace edged by a fine steel railing, and soffits lined in timber with recessed downlights. At street level a horizontal slatted gate and striped entrance steps set a quiet rhythm. Inside, the palette turns richer. The living room is shaped by a layered ceiling with warm cove light and a metal screen inset with blue glass. A bedroom lounge pairs a timber wall, inlaid with fine diagonal lines, with leather tub chairs and copper pendants, and a deep blue upholstered wall repeats the diagonal inlay as a detail.
