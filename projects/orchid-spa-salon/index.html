Interior design

An interior project for The Orchid Spa & Salon. The salon floor lines up styling stations and black wash basins under a stepped ceiling washed in warm yellow light, with a pink-lit feature wall and timber flooring. At the entrance, a tall backlit panel of black veined stone stands beside a carved white bowl of rose petals, beneath a flower-shaped ceiling motif and a crystal chandelier. A narrow waiting corridor of veined stone-effect walls, black seating and plants leads further in, and the spa room is built around a round jacuzzi beneath a curved ceiling edged in blue and violet light, with uplighters grazing a textured wall.
