Sloping Roof Villa

DLF Gardencity, Indore

A villa with a sloping roof at DLF Gardencity, Indore.

A villa at DLF Gardencity, Indore, named for its roofs: in the photograph a broad gable, lined in charcoal with recessed lights, tilts over a white volume and a glass-fronted balcony, beside a stone-clad tower and a garden terrace. The diagonal becomes the thread of the interiors. In the living room, teak-toned slats run on the bias across the television wall and a black metal grille repeats the angle at the window, above a grey marble floor and a geometric rug. The master bedroom carries a full wall of timber chevrons over a grey upholstered headboard in the same zig-zag, softened by teal curtains. The kitchen pairs grey and teal lacquered shutters with a lit yellow backsplash and a timber breakfast counter, and the kids' room has a light timber bunk bed under a triangular lit ceiling.
