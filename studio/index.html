About

phi-Q Architects is a team of architects and interior designers based in Indore. The studio's portfolio spans residential, commercial and institutional projects in Indore and other cities of Madhya Pradesh.

We believe in designing user-friendly, innovative and beautiful spaces which ensure delightful experiences for the people who use them.

People

The architects

Ar. Swarnim Baraskar

Architect · Creative & technical head

Swarnim is a graduate in architecture from Maulana Azad National Institute of Technology (MANIT), Bhopal, and the creative and technical head of the firm.

He is an active member of the Indian Institute of Architects (IIA), Indore Chapter, has represented Team India at the Architects' Tri-Nation Cricket Tournament in Sri Lanka, and has won the NASA–ISOLA Landscape Design Trophy. He is passionate about physics, design, music and cricket.

Process

How a project usually unfolds

A general outline; every brief is different.

  1. Brief

    We listen first: how you live or work, the site, the budget and the time you have.

  2. Concept

    Plans, massing and 3D views to explore the options together.

  3. Design

    The chosen direction is developed into drawings, materials and interiors.

  4. Build

    We stay involved as the drawings become a building.
